    What this is teaching you

    Risk and return travel together

    Bitcorn has the highest expected return on the board. It also has the widest range of outcomes by far. Nothing here offers high returns at low risk, because nothing in the real world does either.

    Compounding needs time, not cleverness

    Money left alone grows on its own growth. Ten years of an ordinary return usually beats a few brilliant trades, which is why the grey benchmark line is so hard to beat.

    Diversification is the one free lunch

    These assets don't move together. Ridgeline often rises when the market falls, and bonds tend to climb during recessions. Holding a mix smooths the ride without giving up much return.

    Fees are small and relentless

    Half a percent per trade sounds trivial. Trade every month for ten years and you have handed over a meaningful slice of everything you earned. Watch the fees tile.

    Cash quietly loses value

    Your savings account pays 2% while prices rise about 2.5% a year. The balance goes up and still buys less. Compare the portfolio value with the "today's money" tile.

    Single stocks carry risk funds don't

    One bad earnings report can take 17% off Nimbus in a month. The index fund holds every company at once, so no single piece of bad news can do that to it.
